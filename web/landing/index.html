GET STARTED

Install once.
Update in place.

Android 8.0 or later. Phone and tablet preview; Android TV testing is still in progress.

  1. Download the signed APK.

    Use the download button on this page.

  2. Open the downloaded file.

    If Android asks, allow installation from your browser, then confirm Install. You can turn that permission off afterwards.

  3. Open MJ Play.

    Use your private-trial invitation to connect. Future public-release updates appear inside the app; Android asks you to confirm installation.

IPHONE & IPAD

MJ Play.
On your Home Screen.

Use the web app in its own window. No App Store download required. An internet connection and your private-trial access are needed.

Open MJ Play in Safari
  1. Open MJ Play in Safari.

    Tap the button here and sign in using the trial access you were given. If this page opened inside another app, open it in Safari first.

  2. Share → Add to Home Screen.

    In Safari, open Share (it may be inside the page menu), then choose Add to Home Screen. Keep Open as Web App enabled if it appears, and tap Add.

  3. Launch the MJ Play icon.

    Open it from the Home Screen. You may be asked for trial access again. Tap Play to start sound. Website updates arrive without installing a new package.

A question before you press play?

Will an update erase my favourites?

Updating the same public app keeps its data. Uninstalling or clearing app data removes local favourites and progress. The Android Studio preview is a separate app from the public release.

Does it need Google Play?

No. Install the signed APK from this page and confirm updates through Android’s installer.

Is this ready for everyone?

This is an early-access build. The streaming service remains invite-only while we test reliability and capacity.

Support and feedback

During the private trial, contact the person who invited you. Include the app version and a description of the problem. Do not send passwords or stream links.
