MJ PLAY · EARLY ACCESS

Your privacy.

Updated 28 October 2026. This notice covers the public web and Android early-access apps. Household profiles and private TV backups are separate.

Saved on your device

Favourites, your list, preferences and viewing progress are saved locally. Updates to the same app preserve them; uninstalling, clearing app data or clearing browser storage can remove them. No cross-device backup is included in this public edition.

Optional diagnostics

If you allow diagnostics, Google Firebase Analytics receives usage events such as section visits, playback starts, failures and buffering durations. Android also uses Firebase Crashlytics for crashes and sanitized playback errors. Custom playback events omit title names, search queries, stream URLs and credentials. Firebase SDKs also process installation/session identifiers and technical device, app and network information.

Choose No thanks to keep optional collection off. You can change your choice in Android’s Privacy menu or the web footer’s Privacy & analytics control. The web app respects Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control.

App configuration and updates

Firebase Remote Config provides feature availability and maintenance settings. Android checks release metadata from the MJ Play server. Updates are downloaded over HTTPS and verified before Android asks for installation confirmation. These operational services work independently of analytics consent.

Streaming and service providers

Catalogue, artwork, subtitle and playback services process requests needed to provide their features. Servers and network providers necessarily receive connection information such as IP addresses. During this private trial, access credentials are stored using Android Keystore and are not included in analytics events.

Questions or deletion

During early access, contact the person who invited you for privacy questions. Disable diagnostics to stop future optional collection. Clearing app/browser data removes local viewing records; it does not by itself delete reports already received by Firebase. Contact the trial organiser to request assistance with collected diagnostics.
